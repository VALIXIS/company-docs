P: ABSTRACT
P: Water is an essential resource for human survival, and maintaining its quality is crucial for public health and safety. However, contaminated water containing excessive dissolved solids can affect its taste, usability, and overall quality. Traditional water testing methods often require manual procedures and may not provide continuous monitoring, making it difficult to identify changes in water quality at an early stage. To address these challenges, this project introduces an automated, low-cost IoT solution: the Aqua Safe – Smart Water Quality Monitoring System.
P: The system is powered by an ESP32 microcontroller integrated with a TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) sensor. It continuously monitors the amount of dissolved solids present in water by measuring TDS values and transmitting the collected readings to the ESP32 for processing. Based on the measured values, the system identifies changes in water quality and provides information about the condition of the tested water. The ESP32 acts as the central processing unit, ensuring efficient sensor data collection and monitoring. This approach helps users observe water quality conditions without depending entirely on traditional manual testing methods.
P: To evaluate the practical  functionality of the system, different water samples can be tested to observe variations in dissolved solids levels. The obtained readings help demonstrate the system's ability to monitor changes in water quality and provide useful information to users. Ultimately, this initiative provides an affordable and efficient smart monitoring solution that promotes water quality awareness and supports better water management in households and other applications.

P: BACKGROUND OF THE SELECTED COMMUNITY AND SOCIAL ISSUE
P: Kakani palem is a village located in Addanki Mandal, Prakasam District, Andhra Pradesh. Water is an essential resource for the daily activities and health of every community. The residents of Kakani palem depend mainly on municipal water supply and water tank supply for their water requirements.
P: Based on the community survey conducted for this project, it was observed that regular drinking water quality testing is not carried out in the community. Some residents also reported problems related to unpleasant taste or smell in the supplied water. These observations indicate the importance of creating awareness about water quality and encouraging regular monitoring.
P: Water quality can be assessed by observing different parameters such as pH, turbidity and temperature. Monitoring these parameters helps identify changes in water characteristics. However, without accessible monitoring methods, residents may find it difficult to understand the condition of their supplied water.
P: Therefore, an affordable and simple IoT-based water quality monitoring approach can help create awareness and provide information about important water parameters.
P: RATIONALE FOR SELECTION OF THE PROJECT
P: The project Aqua Safe – Smart Water Quality Monitoring System was selected to address the need for better awareness and monitoring of water quality in the selected community.
P: During the community survey, the lack of regular drinking water testing and complaints about unpleasant taste or smell highlighted the importance of water quality awareness. This motivated the development of an IoT-based prototype that can monitor important water parameters and display the readings in an understandable format.
P: The project uses an ESP32 microcontroller connected to pH, turbidity, temperature and water level sensors. The collected readings are processed by the controller and displayed on an LCD screen. This provides a simple way to observe water-related parameters without requiring a web application or user interface.
P: The main reasons for choosing this project are:
P: To address the community's need for awareness of drinking water quality.
P: To demonstrate the use of IoT technology for water monitoring.
P: To provide a simple hardware-based monitoring solution.
P: To help residents understand important water quality parameters.
P: To encourage the importance of regular water testing and responsible water management.
P: The prototype is intended for preliminary monitoring and awareness. It does not replace laboratory testing or certify water as safe for drinking.
P: OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT
P: The main objective of Aqua Safe is to design and develop an IoT-based smart water quality monitoring prototype that observes important water parameters and displays the readings through an LCD.
P: The specific objectives are:
P: To develop one working IoT-based water quality monitoring prototype using an ESP32 microcontroller.
P: To integrate four sensors: pH, turbidity, temperature and water level sensors.
P: To monitor and display pH values, turbidity levels, temperature and water level.
P: To process the sensor readings using the ESP32 microcontroller.
P: To display the collected sensor readings on an LCD screen.
P: To provide a basic indication of monitored water conditions based on predefined reference limits.
P: To demonstrate the working of the prototype and explain its functions to community members.
P: To create awareness among residents of Kakani palem about water quality parameters and the importance of regular water testing.
P: To encourage the use of simple IoT technology for better water monitoring and management.

P: IMPLEMENTATION OF AQUA SAFE PROTOTYPE
P: The Aqua Safe prototype works through the integration of four sensors with an ESP32 microcontroller.
P: The pH sensor measures the acidity or alkalinity of water, while the turbidity sensor monitors the cloudiness of water. The temperature sensor measures the water temperature, and the water level sensor monitors the available water level.
P: The ESP32 receives and processes the sensor readings. The monitored values are then displayed on the LCD screen so that users can observe the water-related parameters. A basic status indication can also be provided using predefined reference limits.
P: The complete system is designed as a hardware-based IoT prototype. It does not require a website or mobile application for displaying the readings.
