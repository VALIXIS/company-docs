4. Master Terms of Engagement

4.1 Exhaustive Specifications: The contracted scope is strictly limited to the service modules chosen from the rate card above. Any structural pivots, extra page templates, or custom backend integrations requested during or post-development constitute out-of-scope work, billed at Valixis's standard rate of ₹2,500 / $45 per engineer-hour under a written Change Order.
4.2 Milestone Payment Structure: For standard builds, fees follow the 50-30-20 framework (50% upfront mobilization, 30% on staging demo approval, 20% prior to live DNS cutover). For pilot cohort builds (₹15k-₹25k), payments are structured as 50% mobilization deposit on Day 1 and 50% upon live domain deployment. Work commences solely upon cleared receipt of Milestone 1.
4.3 Client Asset Delay Clause (7-Day Rule): Client shall deliver all brand assets (logos, photography, menu prices, payment keys, DNS access) within seven (7) business days. Any delay by Client automatically extends the target delivery deadline by the same duration. If delays exceed thirty (30) days, Valixis may invoice for completed milestones and place the project on standby.
4.4 Revision Policy: Client is entitled to one (1) consolidated written round of feedback during pilot builds (or two rounds for standard builds), compiled by the designated Single Point of Contact (SPOC).
4.5 Conditional IP Ownership Transfer: Valixis assigns all bespoke design graphics, customized styling, and branding assets to Client strictly upon receipt of 100% full and final payment. Valixis retains sole ownership over its pre-existing code libraries, developer tools, algorithms, 3D shaders, and reusable boilerplates, granting Client a perpetual, royalty-free license for live operational use.
4.6 Zero Custody of Funds: All customer payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, PhonePe, or UPI VPA) must be owned and KYC-verified directly in Client's name. Valixis acts solely as a technical software integrator and at no point holds, custodies, or processes customer funds.
4.7 30-Day Bug-Fix Warranty & Liability Ceiling: Valixis provides a complimentary thirty (30) day warranty starting from live launch to resolve any coding defects inconsistent with agreed specifications. To the maximum extent permitted by law, Valixis's total aggregate liability arising out of this engagement shall not exceed the total fees actually received from Client.
4.8 Kill Fee & Cancellation: The initial 50% mobilization deposit is non-refundable as liquidated damages for committed engineering capacity. If Client terminates for convenience, Client must settle all unbilled work completed to date.
4.9 Governing Law & Jurisdiction: This engagement is governed by the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Hyderabad, Telangana.
