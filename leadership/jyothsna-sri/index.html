Executive Profile

Scaling Technology Adoption & Strategic Operations

Jyothsna Sri is the Co-Founder and Chief Commercial Officer (CCO) of VALIXIS, an independent technology venture founded in 2026. Directing commercial expansion, strategic partnerships, and organizational scaling, Jyothsna plays an essential leadership role in establishing the venture's operational rigor and client relationship frameworks.

She oversees external stakeholder engagements, commercial growth pipelines, and client alignment across all VALIXIS divisions. Her focus is on scaling proprietary platforms with institutional standards, creating sustainable revenue models, and bridging technical capability with direct enterprise value.
