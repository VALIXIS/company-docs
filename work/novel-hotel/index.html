The Operational Challenge

Hospitality, F&B, and specialty retail venues often face steep commercial friction: third-party delivery aggregators charge between 22% and 28% commissions on every transaction, conceal customer phone numbers, and prevent venues from establishing durable customer relationships. Furthermore, slow legacy websites frequently lose 50%+ of mobile visitors before checkout can occur.

The VALIXIS Engineering Solution

VALIXIS engineered a bespoke, ultra-fast mobile-first platform tailored specifically to Novel Hotel & Suites. By implementing direct 1-click UPI payments, frictionless modifier selectors, and real-time automated WhatsApp dispatch, the platform removes intermediary take-rates and delivers an instantaneous ordering experience.
